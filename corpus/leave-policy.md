# Leave policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive **25 days of annual leave** per calendar year, plus public holidays of their country of
employment. Up to **5 unused days** can be carried over into the next year and must be used by 31 March.

## Sick leave
Sick leave is separate from annual leave. A doctor's note is only required for absences longer than **3 consecutive
working days**.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive **20 weeks** of fully paid parental leave; secondary caregivers receive **6 weeks**. Leave
can be taken in up to three blocks within the first year.

## Requesting leave
Leave is requested in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days. Engineers on
an on-call rotation must arrange a swap before their leave is approved.
