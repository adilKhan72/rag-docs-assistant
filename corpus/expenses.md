# Expenses

## What can be claimed
Travel, accommodation and meals on business trips; conference tickets approved in advance; and home-office
equipment up to the annual allowance.

## Limits
- Meals while travelling: up to **USD 75 per day**.
- Hotels: up to USD 220 per night, or USD 300 in London, New York and Tokyo.
- Home-office allowance: **USD 600 per year**, for equipment such as chairs, monitors and desks.

## Submitting
Expenses must be submitted with receipts within **30 days** of the purchase, in the finance portal. Claims over
USD 1,000 need approval from the employee's director before purchase.

## Not reimbursed
Alcohol, upgrades to business class on flights shorter than six hours, and traffic or parking fines.
