# Onboarding

## First week
New engineers get a laptop shipped before their start date and an onboarding buddy from their team. In the first
week they set up their development environment with the `bootstrap.sh` script, complete security training, and ship
a small change to production. That's the "first-week deploy" tradition.

## 30-60-90 plan
Every new hire has a 30-60-90 day plan agreed with their manager in the first week. By day 30 they should have
joined code review; by day 60 they own a small feature end to end; by day 90 they join the on-call rotation as
secondary.

## Accounts
Accounts for GitHub, Slack, PagerDuty and Vault are created by IT through the onboarding ticket. Production access is
never granted in the first 30 days.
